What the figures measure

Statistics Canada’s customs records assign domestic goods to the province where they were grown, mined or manufactured. Destination means the last country known when the goods were exported. Each share divides that destination’s recorded value by the value going to all destinations for the same province, product and period.

Values are current Canadian dollars. Price changes and shipment volumes can both affect the result. A missing destination row contributes no reported value. If a product has no positive total, its shares are unavailable. Product groups overlap with their broader chapters and all merchandise.
